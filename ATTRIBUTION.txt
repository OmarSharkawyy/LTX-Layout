LTX LAYOUT

Original Nuke integration, controls, EXR delivery, and comparison video by
Omar El Sharkawy.

The bundled layout_api.json adapts the official LTX-2.5 Layout to Render
ComfyUI workflow by Lightricks. The API graph was flattened and its inputs
were adapted for Nuke, with a separate EXR output added at runtime.
https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/vfx-post-production/layout-to-render
https://github.com/Lightricks/ComfyUI-LTXVideo
